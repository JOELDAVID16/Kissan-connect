# Kissan Connect: Feature Guide

Kissan Connect is a professional, farm-to-buyer marketplace designed to empower farmers and provide consumers with fresh produce at fair prices. Below are the key features of the platform.

## 1. User Role Management
- **Farmer Profiles:** Farmers can register to list their produce, track orders, and view sales analytics.
- **Buyer Profiles:** Buyers can browse the marketplace, place orders, and bid on premium items.
- **Secure Authentication:** Built-in login/signup system with role-based dashboard redirection.

## 2. Product Marketplace
- **Produce Listings:** Farmers can list products with images, variety, quantity (kg), and price.
- **Advanced Search:** Buyers can search for specific products across the marketplace.
- **Quality Grading:** Products are tagged with quality levels (Low, Medium, High).

## 3. Real-Time Bidding System
- **Bid Placement:** Buyers can place bids on products where bidding is enabled.
- **Countdown Timers:** Interactive timers show remaining time for active auctions.
- **Auto-Order Creation:** When bidding closes, an order is automatically generated for the highest bidder.

## 4. Order & Transaction Management
- **Order Placement:** Seamless ordering process for buyers.
- **Farmer Confirmation:** Farmers can review and "Accept" orders to finalize deals.
- **Contact Sharing:** Once an order is accepted, contact details (email, phone, location) are shared between the parties.
- **Status Tracking:** Orders progress through states: Pending, Accepted, and Completed.

## 5. Direct Communication (Chat)
- **Person-to-Person Messaging:** Farmers and buyers can chat directly to discuss products, delivery, or pricing.
- **Real-Time Notification:** Unread message indicators and timestamped conversation history.

## 6. AI-Powered Insights
- **Smart Price Suggestions:** Farmers receive suggested price ranges based on real-time market data scraping.
- **Demand Indicators:** Products are analyzed to show market demand levels (High/Medium/Low).

## 7. Analytics Dashboard
- **Revenue Tracking:** Visual bar charts showing income by product.
- **Order Distribution:** Pie charts illustrating the breakdown of pending vs. accepted orders.
- **Key Metrics:** Quick view of total income, average order value, and listed products.

## 8. Localization & Global Support
- **Multi-Language UI:** Fully translated interface supporting:
  - English
  - Hindi (हिन्दी)
  - Tamil (தமிழ்)
  - Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)
  - Malayalam (മലയാളம்)

## 9. Interactive Mapping
- **Location Selection:** Use of Leaflet and OpenStreetMap for product origins and delivery addresses.
- **Geolocation:** Automatic coordinate capturing for accurate logistics.
