# 🌾 Kissan Connect

**Kissan Connect** is a Django-based agricultural e-commerce platform that connects farmers directly with buyers through a digital marketplace. The platform enables farmers to showcase and sell their agricultural products while allowing buyers to discover and purchase products conveniently.

The project aims to simplify agricultural trading, improve market accessibility, and help farmers make better pricing decisions through technology.

## Features

- **Farmer & Buyer Authentication** – Separate registration and login for farmers and buyers.
- **Product Management** – Farmers can add, update, and manage agricultural products.
- **Buy & Sell Marketplace** – Buyers can browse and purchase products directly from farmers.
- **AI-Based Price Prediction** – Helps estimate suitable prices for agricultural products.
- **Product Search** – Search and discover available products.
- **Chat System** – Enables communication between farmers and buyers.
- **Product Reviews** – Buyers can share feedback about products.
- **Flexible Delivery Options** – Farmers can choose their preferred delivery methods.

## Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Python, Django
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Database:** SQLite
- **Additional Technology:** Machine Learning for price prediction

## Project Objective

To build a digital platform that bridges the gap between farmers and consumers, reduces dependency on traditional intermediaries, and promotes a more accessible and technology-driven agricultural marketplace.
